About me

I just graduated as a student studying Creative Technology and Design, where I explored the space between visual design, engineering, and interactive systems. I've always been creative and enjoy making beautiful things, but I'm also deeply drawn to logic and structure. I like knowing there's a right solution, or at least a better one. This field has allowed me to combine both.

I approach every project function-first. If something isn't usable, it isn't successful. I care about clarity, organization, and thoughtful interaction. I aim to balance structure with play, ensuring that the work I create is both usable and enjoyable.

I'm detail-oriented and love refining interactions until they feel intuitive. What frustrates me most is bad design that gets in the way of the user experience, and I enjoy finding creative solutions to real-world problems that make life easier.
